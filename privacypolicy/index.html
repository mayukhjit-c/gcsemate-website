Plain-English Summary

GCSEMate stores the information needed to let you sign in, save study progress, sync notes and flashcards, manage billing or premium access, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how GCSEMate ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our website and services.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is GCSEMate. Contact: admin@gcsemate.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: display name, email address, subscription tier, role, allowed subjects.
  • Saved Study Data: notes, flashcards, calendar events, planner items, grade tracker entries, and preferences you choose to store.
  • Usage Data: actions within the app, pages viewed, timestamps, and basic operational analytics used to improve reliability.
  • Device/Technical Data: IP address, browser type, approximate device information, and error logs automatically provided by your browser or service providers.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: used to maintain sessions, remember UI settings, and keep the service working consistently.

3. Sources of Information

Information is provided directly by you or generated when you use the service (e.g., saving preferences). We may also receive limited technical data from service providers.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the service (authentication, rendering content, saving preferences).
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Communicate with you about updates or important service notices.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. Legal Bases

Under UK/EU GDPR, our processing bases include performance of a contract, legitimate interests (e.g., service improvement, security), and consent where applicable.

6. Sharing and Processors

We use third-party processors to provide the service, including Firebase for authentication and database services, Google services for hosted content and embeds, and payment or operational tooling where required. These providers process data under their own terms and security controls.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account continuity, investigate abuse, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion at any time via account controls or by contacting us.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards.

10. Your Rights

  • Access, correction, deletion, and portability of your data.
  • Restriction or objection to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK ICO or your local authority.

11. Children's Privacy

GCSEMate is designed for students. If you are under 13, use only with parent/guardian consent as required by local laws.

12. Cookies

We use cookies essential to the operation of the service. Your browser may allow you to block cookies, but the service may not work properly if you do.

13. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect legal or operational changes. We will post updates in the app and update the date below.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact: admin@gcsemate.com.
